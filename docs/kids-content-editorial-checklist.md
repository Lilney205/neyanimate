# Kids' Educational Anime: Editorial Checklist

Use this checklist while drafting family and children's titles in Studio. Optional minimum-age recommendations are editorial guidance only; they are not official ratings, safety certifications, or a substitute for local legal review.

## Before production

- [ ] State one primary learning outcome in plain language.
- [ ] Add two or three observable learning goals (for example, “count a set of objects from 1 to 10”).
- [ ] Choose an optional minimum-age recommendation and have educators/caregivers review the vocabulary, pacing, examples, and expected prior knowledge.
- [ ] Keep the synopsis and parent note accurate to the finished episode; do not promise educational outcomes that have not been reviewed.
- [ ] Plan captions/subtitles, legible numerals, audio clarity, and a visual alternative for essential spoken instructions.
- [ ] Verify that music, artwork, voices, and any third-party material are owned or licensed for streaming.

## Before publishing

- [ ] Watch the complete final encode, including captions and audio, on a phone and desktop.
- [ ] Confirm each stated learning goal actually appears in the video.
- [ ] Check for age-appropriate language, frightening imagery, unsafe imitation, advertising, and unexpected external links.
- [ ] Write a short caregiver note with a discussion prompt or suggested offline activity; label fictional previews clearly.
- [ ] Confirm the approved video asset is processed and attached to the intended draft.
- [ ] Have a second reviewer sign off; record the review outside this prototype until an editorial review/audit workflow is added.

## Child privacy and operations

- Do not request a child's name, precise location, contact information, school, photo, or other personal data in the content or playback experience.
- This project does not yet provide child profiles, parental consent, age verification, or a legally reviewed kids' privacy program. Do not collect data from child viewers or launch a child-directed service until applicable privacy/advertising laws and jurisdiction-specific requirements have been reviewed by qualified counsel.
- Avoid behavioral advertising and tracking for child-directed experiences. Review analytics, cookies, third-party embeds, and retention settings before public release.
- A parent note and a minimum-age recommendation do not make a title “certified safe” or guarantee learning results.
